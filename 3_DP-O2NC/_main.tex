\chapter{Differentially Private Online-to-Non-Convex}
\label{chapter:dp-o2nc}
\thispagestyle{myheadings}

\paragraph{Abstract}
We introduce a new zeroth-order algorithm for private stochastic optimization on nonconvex and nonsmooth objectives.
Given a dataset of size $M$, our algorithm ensures $(\alpha,\alpha\rho^2/2)$-R\'enyi differential privacy and finds a $(\delta,\epsilon)$-stationary point so long as $M=\tilde\Omega\left(\frac{d}{\delta\epsilon^3} + \frac{d^{3/2}}{\rho\delta\epsilon^2}\right)$.
This matches the optimal complexity of its non-private zeroth-order analog. 
Notably, although the objective is not smooth, we have privacy ``for free'' whenever $\rho \ge \sqrt{d}\epsilon$.
